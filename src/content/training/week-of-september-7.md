115 miles run. Mon. 15, Tues. 17, Wed. 23, Thurs. 17, Fri. 10, Sat. 17, Sun. 15

The iron feels warmer than ever.

Of course, that implies that now is a better time than ever to strike. Conventional and rational coaching marathon philosophies all land on the idea of culminating a build with one grand attempt. However, I believe that philosophy neglects momentum and the competitive spirit of racing.

In that spirit, I aim to partake in a modern Bill Rodgers-inspired fall racing calendar. I mean, the dude was ripping 150-mile weeks and 5 marathons a year in shoes with less cushion than a sock. Whether it be Bill Rodgers on the roads or Mike Roche at Rutgers, I think a few of the beneficial aspects of a grindstone approach shouldn’t have been forgotten with time.

History lesson aside, I’m excited to race. Philadelphia Distance Run 13.1 on September 20th and the Wineglass Marathon on October 4th. For the half marathon, there’s no reason why I can’t pop a big one. The best racing plan is no plan, so I’ll appreciate the opportunity to race in an elite field and try to scalp as many professional runners as possible. And I wouldn’t mind running sub-1:05 if the weather permits.

As for the following race, that’ll be an experience affair. I’ve intentionally neglected the long run in recent weeks, so I will go in with dampened expectations. That said, with the 1300 miles I’ve logged in the last 12 weeks, and some fresh legs, I’ll at least try for the course record of 2:16:58.

Too much rambling this week.

TLDR: Strike while the iron is hot, carpe diem, or whatever expression speaks most to you. Onwards.
