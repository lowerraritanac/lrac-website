## Week Recap

115 miles run. Mon. 17, Tues. 20, Wed. 18, Thurs. 15, Fri. 16, Sat. 18, Sun. 10

More volume and intensity than planned. Feeling good is a double-edged sword and shields the mind from the consequences associated with going for broke.

So, I realize there is no point in chasing. Be it chasing mileage, chasing times, or chasing results. Equally important, there is no point in fearing those same metrics. After all, aspiring to the metrics of how someone achieved a desirable result is meaningless without the context of individuality.

Alongside my current training thoughts is the curse, or gift, of improvement. I know it to be a gift, but it is quite ironic. Ironic because 7 months ago I first solidified the OTQ as an end goal and I’d hang up the shoes; 7 weeks ago, I began writing training recaps as if it were some unattainable goal; and 7 weeks from now, I have already forgotten that as the goal. I still desire it, but now I want to be a competitor on the line in 2028, which will require some leaps.

Again, there are no times or results to chase, but I know one thing for certain: I must *“Train like a madman”*
