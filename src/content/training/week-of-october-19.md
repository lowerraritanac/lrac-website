## Week Recap

123 miles run. Mon. 18, Tues. 24, Wed. 18, Thurs. 17, Fri. 16, Sat. 20, Sun. 10

Another great week. Another mundane one. Building volume, building sessions, and establishing a higher goal post than the last. But here’s a new training thought of my future instead of an all too familiar training recap.

***Zig when the masses are zagging.***

In running, zagging is controlled volume, following the NCAA system, spending years cultivating a career on the track, then juicing the final years on the roads if you really love it. I respect that, but I am not built for that. If the reasonable path forward is to follow convention, I will be a middling sub-elite who is proud and steadfast in the consensus of the masses. It is fair to say that in this context, being 22 with goals too large to speak is a zig.

Now, these figurative runners I speak of are far better than me. These conventional opinions form the American marathoning system, and again, I respect that. These runners can beat me to a pulp without taking a breath. They are fitter than me, they know more than me, and they’ve experienced more than me. That is, for now.

What if I abandon convention? What if I look to the 1970s greats? What if I look to the hundreds of Japanese, Kenyans, and Ethiopians capable of demolishing the West today?

I will likely find that a zig elsewhere is a zag here. I may also find that conventional opinions are just the fundamentals of this event; then I will be proven wrong. So be it.

In truth, all of us want to be the best. Nobody can say it, but we all believe it and dream it.

Time will tell if this is all rambling, and time will tell if it is about running.
