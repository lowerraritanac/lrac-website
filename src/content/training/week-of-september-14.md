85 miles run. Mon. 16, Tues. 16, Wed. 10, Thurs. 14, Fri. 8, Sat. 5, Sun. 16

Base hits.

That’s the objective, but damn, do I want to go yard.

1:06:05 for 14th in a deep field at the Philadelphia Distance Run this morning. Middling result, but learned a great deal in terms of how to race on the roads today, so I’ll take it. The main lesson for me is that humans are remarkably talented at managing pain, but even more so at avoiding it. While I didn’t go into a dark place today, I know that I am capable of doing so on the roads when it matters later in the year.

My next aim is learning the marathon in Corning, NY, at the Wineglass Marathon on October 4th, where I’ll make it very simple for myself. Run to win the thing, practice fueling a ton, and hopefully dig myself into a chasm in which to crawl out of. I yearn for the result, but then, similar to today, I want to execute even more.

So I’ll lay out my race plan:

1. Go with whatever pacer there is, hopefully 2:16:00 pace.
2. Race to win, then race for every second.
3. Deal with the repercussions of this plan,

No insane training for a couple of weeks; yet I’m a tad excited for that to come back around.
