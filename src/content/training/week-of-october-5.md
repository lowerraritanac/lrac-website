## Week Recap

75 miles run. Mon. OFF, Tues. 7, Wed. 8, Thurs. 15, Fri. 16, Sat. 14, Sun. 15

Surprisingly responded well to the race last week and ready to put it in the rearview mirror. To recap the boring nature of blah, blah, training, blah, blah…

Similar to my mindset a week ago, I’ve picked up the optimism itch, and I don’t fancy letting it go. Optimism of two parts:

1. The process is as fulfilling as it’s ever been, and I’d like to keep that going regardless of the result.
2. This is the more nuanced one. I’m starting to believe that to be nationally competitive in the marathon, you don’t need to be *that fast*. Back-of-the-napkin math inbound: Yomif Kejelcha is unbelievably fast with the most absurd range known to man, but his 10k pace is “only” 4:15 pace; marathon pace is 4:33. That differential is quite small in the context of my goals, where my current 10k best lies at just under 4:50 pace; and current marathon pace is just above 5:10. Here’s where my logic may become quite illogical, yet I believe it and will train like it. 10k at 4:40 pace is psychologically attainable with my foot speed, so I cannot reason that sub 5:00 for a marathon, or 2:11, isn’t attainable.

The problem of talent for the next goal, after the next, after the next… does not concern me today, and for now, I am beginning to dream about what I will do once I have a spot in the arena.

But for now, I will focus on earning it.
