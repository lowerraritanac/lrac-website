122 miles run. Mon. 23, Tues. 17, Wed. 17, Thurs. 17, Fri. 17, Sat. 17, Sun. 22

First week in the corporate world. As visualized by the four 17-mile days, I take a liking to sticking to a routine. Eventually I’m sure this monotony will be stale, but four days- that’s gravy.

That’s beside the point. My primary objective in summarizing each week in my build towards the marathon is to keep the main thing the main thing, when it is time for it to be the main thing. Meaning, when the running shoes are laced, I better be running. This week the theme was consistency. 13 runs, 10 easy, 3 workouts. Nothing too flashy aside from a nice Sunday session, besides the lively Labor Day weekend crowd in the Park.

> 11 miles at 5:11 pace. 4 miles - 5:03, 1 mile float - 5:48, 3 miles - 5:03, 1 mile float - 5:50, 2 miles - 5:02

Nothing screams freak like ripping through a crowded public park in half tights and a 40-year-old, beat-to-shit, kayak guide hat. Strangely, that vibe encompasses the most productive training hours. Hours where the ability to give a shit is gone, when feel dictates pace, and when a training plan is built on a whim. It’s a nice illustration of our autonomy to do whatever we wish, however insignificant it may be.

Anyways, this week surely got me one step closer to my grand goal. And one step closer to my cubicle-backed demise. Onwards.
